Gridlock Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026.

In short: The Gridlock does not collect any personal data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. The game has no servers of its own and sends nothing to us.

1. Who is responsible

The Gridlock (shown as Gridlock on your Home Screen) is published by Hamdi Jomaa, entrepreneur individuel (EI), a French sole trader, 41 B rue des Roches, 45210 Ferrières-en-Gâtinais, France, SIREN 927 699 579 (“we”, “us”). Contact for anything in this policy: hamdijomaa.x@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Nothing. We do not collect, receive, sell or share any personal data. The Gridlock contains no advertising, analytics or third-party tracking code, and it does not use the advertising identifier.

3. What stays on your device

Your progress (the locks you have cracked, your CRED, rank and HEAT streak, Chips, boosters and cosmetics, your records and what you have bought) and your settings are saved on your device so the game can remember them.

4. Game Center and saved games

If you sign in to Game Center, the game uses it in two ways:

  • It keeps a copy of your progress as a Game Center saved game, which Apple stores in your own iCloud account so your progress can follow you from one device to another. It also reads your own bests from its leaderboards to restore your progress. We cannot see or access your saved game.
  • It sends your scores, achievements, activities and challenge progress to Game Center, which shows them to you and, depending on your Game Center settings, to other players (for example your nickname and score on a leaderboard).

Game Center and iCloud are run by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in, and you can manage your Game Center profile and visibility in the Settings app › Game Center.

5. In-app purchases

In-app purchases are sold by Apple through the App Store, and Apple handles the payment. We never see your name, e-mail address or payment details. On your device, the game only receives Apple’s confirmation of which item was bought, so that it can deliver it. Apple gives us sales reports that do not identify you. Ask to Buy requests are handled entirely by Apple.

6. Crash reports and App Store statistics

If you chose to share analytics with app developers (Settings › Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us anonymous crash reports and usage statistics. They do not identify you, and we use them only to fix problems. You can turn this off at any time in the same place.

7. Children

Because The Gridlock collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either. Parents can approve or block in-app purchases with Ask to Buy and Screen Time.

8. Your rights

As we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct, export or delete. Deleting the app removes the progress saved on your device; your Game Center saved game and your other Game Center data are tied to your Apple Account and managed by Apple. If you have any question, write to hamdijomaa.x@gmail.com. You may also complain to the French data protection authority, the CNIL, or to the authority in your country.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date. If The Gridlock ever started collecting data, we would say so here and in the App Store before it happened.
